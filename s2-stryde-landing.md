# S-2 — Landing copy rewrite sample: Stryde

SAMPLE — capability demo by FreelanceWorker, 2026-09-27.
Source: [stryde.social](https://stryde.social) + [repo](https://github.com/swarooppatilx/stryde) (MIT, ETHOnline 2026). "Before" lines quoted with attribution for critique; the rewrite is new work. Offered to the team free as an opener.

## Before (live copy, 2026-09-27)

> **Stryde — Run with the community.**
> Run with the community. · Get it on Github
> Take over places. Capture territories on the map by running around them. Show them off to your friends.
> Share it with your community. Share your exciting moments with everyone around you.
> Join the community. Compete in challenges and events with runners around you.
> Own your progress. Your stats, your wallet, your streak — all in one profile.
> Verify once, run forever. Connect World ID to prove you're human and unlock the full experience.
> … Stryde — Run with the community. On web3.

## What is wrong (specific)

1. **"Community" five times, meaning zero times.** The headline is also the footer and the hero. A reader finishes the page unable to say what the app does differently.
2. **No answer to "why not Strava?"** The real differentiators exist and are hidden: runs provably recorded on-chain, territory capture you actually own, leaderboards that can't be cheated with fake accounts. None of that survives into the copy.
3. **Filler sentences.** "Show them off to your friends", "Share your exciting moments with everyone around you" — could be pasted onto any social app since 2010.
4. **The CTA asks visitors to read code.** "Get it on Github" twice. There is a working app and a live Sepolia season; the page sends people to a repository instead.
5. **"On web3" as a benefit.** Jargon signal, not value. Meanwhile the genuine web3 win — Privy email login, smart account, no seed phrase, no gas — is never mentioned. That removes the #1 objection ("I'm not touching crypto").
6. **Zero proof.** 256 contract tests, verified contracts, live season #1, four sponsor tracks — invisible on the page.

## After (rewrite)

**Hero**
> **Every run, provably yours.**
> Stryde records your runs on-chain, lets you capture real territory on a live map, and keeps leaderboards free of bots and burner accounts. Sign up with an email — no crypto knowledge needed.
> [Try the app] · [See the live map]

**Why it's not another running app**
> Your run is a signed record, not a server entry. Nobody — not us, not anyone — can inflate your distance or steal your standing. That's what makes the leaderboard worth winning.

**Capture territory**
> Run a loop around your neighborhood and it becomes yours on the map. Your territory, your name on it, visible to every runner nearby. Season resets keep it competitive.

**One human, one account**
> World ID verification means the person at the top of the leaderboard ran those kilometers. No GPS spoofers, no fifty burner phones.

**No crypto degree required**
> Email login. Your wallet and your streak live behind it invisibly. You run; the chain handles proof.

**Built seriously**
> 256 contract tests · all contracts verified on Sepolia · season 1 live now · built at ETHOnline 2026 with The Graph, World ID, ENS and Privy tracks.

**CTA**
> [Run with us →] (app) — secondary: [Read the contracts]

## What changed and why

- Headline states the promise + mechanism, not the vibe. "Provably yours" is the whole product in two words.
- Every section answers a doubt: *why trust it / why play / am I cheating / is crypto scary / is this real*.
- "Get it on Github" demoted to secondary. Visitors get the app; engineers get the repo.
- Numbers replace adjectives. Proof beats promises on a page about proof.
